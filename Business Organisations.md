### 1. Define Business, Commerce, Trade, Auxiliaries To Trade 

## Business
A **business** is an economic activity that involves the production, purchase, or sale of goods and services with the aim of earning a profit.

## Commerce
**Commerce** refers to all activities involved in the distribution of goods and services from producers to consumers.

## Trade
**Trade** is the buying and selling of goods and services between individuals, businesses, or countries.

------------

### 2. Types Of Trade
- 🏠 Internal Trade
  - Wholesale Trade
  - Retail Trade
- 🌍 External Trade
  - Import Trade
  - Export Trade
  - Entrepot Trade

-------

### 3. Mention the Auxiliaries To Trade
These are services that help trade take place smoothly.

### Examples
- 🏦 Banking
- 🚚 Transport
- 🛡️ Insurance
- 📢 Advertising
- 📦 Warehousing
- 📡 Communication

---


### 4. List Business Objectives
- **Profit Making** - To earn income and sustain operations.

- **Customer Satisfaction** - To provide quality products and services.

- **Growth And Expansion** - To increase market share and business size.

- **Survival** - To remain in operation despite competition.

- **Market Leadership** - To become the leading business in a market.

- **Social Responsibility** - To contribute positively to society and the environment.

---

### 5. Characteristics Of Business Activities 
- **🔄 Economic Activity** - Business involves production and distribution of goods and services.

- **💰 Profit Motive** - The primary objective is earning profit.

- **⚠️ Risk And Uncertainty** - Business faces possible losses and changing conditions.

- **🔁 Continuous Process** - Business activities occur regularly.

- **👥 Customer Satisfaction** - Activities focus on meeting customer needs.

- **💵 Investment Requirement** - Capital is needed to start and operate a business.

- **📦 Production Or Exchange** - Business involves producing or exchanging goods and services.

---

### 6. Types Of Industry

#### ⛏️ Extractive Industry
Obtains natural resources from the earth.

**Examples:**
- Mining
- Fishing
- Forestry

#### 🌾 Genetic Industry
Produces living organisms and plants.

**Examples:**
- Farming
- Poultry Keeping
- Fish Farming

#### 🏭 Manufacturing Industry
Converts raw materials into finished goods.

**Examples:**
- Car Manufacturing
- Food Processing
- Textile Production

#### 🏗️ Construction Industry
Involves building infrastructure.

**Examples:**
- Roads
- Bridges
- Buildings

---

### 7. Causes Of Business Risks 
- **Changes In Demand** - Consumer preferences may change.

- **Government Policies** - New laws and regulations may affect operations.

- **Natural Disasters** - Floods, droughts, earthquakes, and fires can disrupt business.

- **Competition** - Rivals may attract customers away.

- **Economic Changes**
Inflation, recession, and interest rate changes may affect profitability.

- **Technological Changes**
New technology may make products obsolete.

- **Human Errors**
Mistakes by employees or management can cause losses.


---

### 8. 🌟 Scanning 🌟

### 📌 Meaning Of Environmental Scanning

Environmental scanning is the process of gathering, analyzing, and interpreting information about factors that affect a business.


### b)  Why Scan The Environment 

### 🔍 Identify Opportunities
Helps businesses discover new markets and customer needs.

### ⚠️ Detect Threats
Allows businesses to prepare for potential challenges.

### 🎯 Improve Planning
Supports better decision-making.

### 📈 Enhance Competitiveness
Helps businesses remain competitive.

### 🔄 Adapt To Change
Allows businesses to respond quickly to environmental changes.

---

### 9.  Components Of Business Environment 
- 📌 Micro Environment
- t consists of factors that directly affect a business.

- Customers
People who buy the products or services.

- Competitors
Other businesses offering similar products.

- Suppliers
Provide raw materials and resources.

- Intermediaries
Assist in distribution and sales.

- Employees
Workers who perform business activities.

- Shareholders
Owners who invest capital in the business.

---

### b) 📌 Macro Environment

The macro environment consists of broader forces that affect all businesses.

- 💹 Economic Factors
Inflation, unemployment, and interest rates.

- 🏛️ Political Factors
Government stability and policies.

- ⚖️ Legal Factors
Laws governing business activities.

- 💻 Technological Factors
Advances in technology and innovation.

- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Social Factors
Lifestyle, values, and social trends.

- 🌍 Cultural Factors
Beliefs, customs, and traditions.

- 📊 Demographic Factors
Population size, age, and income levels.

- 🌱 Environmental Factors
Climate change, pollution, and sustainability concerns.

# 🏪 1. Small Businesses

## 📌 Definition

A **small business** is an independently owned and operated enterprise that has small scale of operations, low investment, little workforce, and small sales volume compared to large businesses.

### 🏘️ Local Examples
- Retail shops
- Salons and barbershops
- Restaurants and cafés
- Small-scale manufacturers
- Cyber cafés
- Grocery stores (kiosks)

---

## 📏 Measurement of Small Businesses

Small businesses are commonly measured in terms of:

### 1.👥 Number of Employees
The total workforce employed by the business.

### 2.💰 Total Investment
The amount of capital invested in the business.

### 3.📈 Sales Turnover
The total revenue generated through sales during a given period.

---

## 🌍 Role of Small Businesses in Development

- Small businesses contribute to economic and social development by:

### 1. Creating Employment
They provide jobs and reduce unemployment.

### 2. Poverty Reduction
They generate income for owners and employees.

### 3. Economic Growth
They increase production and contribute to GDP.

### 4. Encouraging Innovation
They introduce new products and business ideas.

### 5. Regional Development
They promote development in rural and urban areas.

### 6. Resource Utilization
They make productive use of local resources.

---

## ⚠️ Problems Facing Small Businesses

### 1.💸 Lack of Capital
Limited access to loans and financing.

### 2.📊 Poor Management Skills
Business owners may lack managerial knowledge.

### 3.🏪 High Competition
Competition from larger firms can be challenging.

### 4.🏛️ Government Regulations
Licensing and taxation requirements may be difficult to meet.

### 5.⚡ Inadequate Infrastructure
Poor roads, electricity, or internet access can affect operations.

### 6.📉 Market Uncertainty
Changes in consumer demand may reduce sales.

# 💡 2. Entrepreneurship

## 📌 Definition

**Entrepreneurship** is the process of identifying a business opportunity, organizing resources, taking risks, and starting and managing a business venture to earn a profit.

---

## ⭐ Importance of Entrepreneurship

### 1.💼 Job Creation
Creates employment opportunities.

### 2.📈 Economic Growth
Contributes to national income and wealth creation.

### 3.💡 Innovation
Encourages development of new products and services.

### 4.🌱 Improved Living Standards
Raises income levels and quality of life.

### 5.🌍 Community Development
Supports local and regional development.

### 6.🏆 Wealth Creation
Generates profits and enhances economic prosperity.

---

## 👤 Characteristics of Entrepreneurs (State & Explain)

### 1️⃣ Risk-Taking
Willingness to face uncertainty and challenges.

### 2️⃣ Innovative
Creates new ideas, products, or methods.

### 3️⃣ Self-Confident
Believes in personal abilities and decisions.

### 4️⃣ Goal-Oriented
Focuses on achieving specific objectives.

### 5️⃣ Persistent
Does not give up easily when faced with obstacles.

### 6️⃣ Leadership Skills
Able to guide and motivate others.

### 7️⃣ Decision-Making Ability
Makes timely and effective decisions.

### 8️⃣ Opportunity Seeking
Identifies and exploits business opportunities.

# 🛒 Week 4 : E-Commerce
### 1. 🌐 Define E-Commerce
  - E-commerce refers to the production, advertising, sale and distribution of products via the electronic network.
    - 🀄 电子商务 — diànzǐ shāngwù = E-commerce

-----------------

### 2. 📱 Channels of E-Commerce
  - 🌐 Internet
  - 📱 Mobile devices
    
    - 🀄 互联网 — hùliánwǎng = Internet

--------------

### 3. 💳 Modes of Payment in E-Commerce
- Cash
- Plastic bank cards
- PayPal
- Amazon Pay
- Apple Pay
- Stripe
- EFT — Electronic Funds Transfer

  - 🀄 支付 — zhīfù = Payment

---------------------

### 4. 🏢 State the Forms/Types of E-Commerce Transactions
- Business to Business - all transactions between a company and its suppliers
- Business to Consumer
- Conumer to Consumer - electronic markets formed by Web-based auctions
- Government to Business- all transactions between a company and government agencies.

  - 🀄 企业 — qǐyè = Business
  - 🀄 客户 — kèhù = Customer
  - 🀄 政府 — zhèngfǔ = Government

------------------

### 5. 🎯 Objectives of E-Commerce
- 📢 Raise awareness of potential opportunities and threats.
- 🌍 Expand customer reach.
- 💰 Reduce cost to serve customers.
- 🤝 Develop business relations.
- ⭐ Create competence and unique customer experience.

  - 🀄 目标 — mùbiāo = Objective
  - 🀄 机会 — jīhuì = Opportunity
  - 🀄 威胁 — wēixié = Threat

------------

### 6. 🏗️ Requirements for E-Commerce
- Policies to facilitate e-commerce.
- Telecommunication infrastructure.
- Legal framework that develops trust in e-commerce transactions.
- Financial environment that facilitates technological development.
- Training that promotes e-commerce.
- Access to inexpensive, reliable internet services.

  - ⚖️🧑‍⚖️ 法律 — fǎlǜ = Law
  - 🎓📚 培训 — péixùn = Training
  - 🛡️✨ 可靠 — kěkào = Reliable

### 8. ✅ State the Advantages of E-Commerce
- 📣 Reduced marketing and advertising expenses.
- 🌍 Unlimited marketplace and business access, extending the customer base.
- 📞 Reduced unnecessary phone calls and mails.
- 🤝 Eliminates middlemen by selling directly to the customer.
- 🎧 Reduces customer service and sales support.
- 💬 Better way of dealing with dealers and customers.
- 🔐 Secure payment system.
- 🌎 Promotes products globally.
- 🚚 Reduced costs on transportation and warehousing.

  - 🀄 全球 — quánqiú = Global
  - 🀄 产品 — chǎnpǐn = Product
  - 🀄 安全 — ānquán = Security/Safety

-----------

### 9. ❌ Disadvantages of E-Commerce
- Bad sites; may take customers' money.
- No guarantee of product quality.
- Mechanical failures; can cause unpredictable effects on total processes.
- Limited direct customer-to-company interaction; can affect customer loyalty.
- Attacks by hackers.
- Delayed orders

  - 🀄 黑客 — hēikè = Hacker
  - 🀄 攻击 — gōngjī = Attack
  - 🀄 客户忠诚度 — kèhù zhōngchéngdù = Customer loyalty

 ----------
 
### 10. Challenges facing E-Commerce - find solution for each

- Borderless customers want customized, personalized goods & services.
- Difficult to get customer loyalty and trust.
- Complications in return and refund services.
- Advanced technology is needed to make purchases.
- Constant innovation to remain competitive.
